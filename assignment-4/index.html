About Dr. Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a renowned Indian scientist, author, and the 11th President of India. He made significant contributions to India's space and defence programmes.

He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He was widely loved and respected for his simple lifestyle, inspiring personality, and dedication to education and young people.

Major Achievements

  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Made important contributions to India's space programme.
  • Played a major role in India's missile development programme.
  • Received the Padma Bhushan and Padma Vibhushan.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna in 1997.
